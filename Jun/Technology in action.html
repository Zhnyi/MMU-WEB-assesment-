Future Technologies

What did IT do to enhance our technology over the past few years?
Well, alot!

Internet, networks and Education

Grid Modernization

AMI 2.0 (advanced metering infrastructure) paired with private 5G/LTE can improve load forecasting and help manage increasing electricity demand from AI data centers.

Direct Air Capture (DAC)

Direct Air Capture removes CO₂ from the atmosphere for deep underground storage or for use in producing synthetic fuels.

Artificial Photosynthesis

Artificial photosynthesis uses sunlight to convert carbon dioxide into storable solar fuels.

Life Sciences & Healthcare

Healthcare

Unified platforms combine gene editing, synthetic biology, and personalized medicine to develop new biological solutions and treatments.

Advanced Remediation

Nanostructured materials can be designed to target persistent pollutants in water and soil, including PFAS.

Smart Aeraulic Systems

AI-managed ventilation systems can balance pathogen protection with energy efficiency in places such as hospitals and schools.

Daily Life

Communication

People use emails, messaging apps, and video calls to stay in touch across long distances.

Finance an Banking

Automated teller machines (ATMs) and debit cards let users manage money without visiting a physical bank branch.

Learning

Students use online classes, digital textbooks, and virtual collaboration tools to study from anywhere.

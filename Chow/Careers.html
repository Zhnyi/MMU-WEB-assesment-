Careers - Google Workspace

3 IT Job Opportunities

System Admin

1. Google Workspace Admin

What they do: They manage all the Google accounts in a company or school. They create new user accounts, keep files safe, set up passwords, and control who can access what.

Creating Users Security Managing Passwords
Developer

2. Apps Script Developer

What they do: They write simple code using Google Apps Script to automate boring tasks. For example, automatically sending emails when a Google Form is submitted.

Google Apps Script Basic Coding Automation
Support & Training

3. IT Support Specialist

What they do: They help people who run into problems while using Google Workspace tools. They also train staff on how to use Docs, Sheets, and Drive properly.

Helpdesk Troubleshooting Teaching Others

Certifications & Training Courses

📜

Google Professional Collaboration Engineer

Who makes it: Google Cloud

What it is: A famous official certification that proves you know how to set up and manage Google Workspace for big companies.

🏅

Google IT Support Professional Certificate

Who makes it: Coursera / Google

What it is: A beginner-friendly online course that teaches you the basics of IT, computer networks, and fixing tech problems.

🏫

Google Certified Educator (Level 1 & 2)

Who makes it: Google for Education

What it is: A certificate meant for teachers or trainers to show they know how to use Google tools in the classroom.

Definition of Google Workspace

Google Workspace is a cloud-based productivity, communication, and collaboration platform developed by Google for individuals, schools, and businesses.

Instead of installing standalone desktop software, Google Workspace brings together creation tools, storage, and communication apps directly inside your web browser and mobile devices.

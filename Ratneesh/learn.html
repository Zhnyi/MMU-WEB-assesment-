Learn: IT Careers

What are IT Careers?

IT careers refer to professional roles in Information Technology, encompassing jobs that involve managing, developing, and supporting computer systems, software, networks, and data. Information Technology, or often called as IT, is a broad feild that focuses on the use of software computer systems, networking devices and data management, which processes, stores, retrives and transmits information. Careers in IT includes positions that widely ranges from technical supports and network administration to software development, cybersecurity, cloud computing and data analysis.

Professional in IT may work on tasks such as programming, maintaining network, managinbg databases, troubleshooting, hardware and software issues, and implementing security measures.

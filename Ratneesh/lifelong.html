Lifelong Learning

🚀What I Learned Independently While Completing This Project?

While completing this project, I independently learned how to design and develop a multi-page website using HTML and CSS. I explored different webpage layouts, worked with navigation menus, inserted and styled images, customized fonts and colours, and used CSS Flexbox to organize content. Through trial and error, I also learned how to solve common web development problems such as broken image links, layout issues, and formatting inconsistencies.



💡Why the Selected Topic Is Important

The topic of IT Careers, Professional Certifications, and Future Technologies is important because technology continues to shape the way people work, communicate, and solve problems. Understanding emerging career opportunities and technology trends helps students prepare for future jobs and remain competitive in the digital workplace. Professional certifications and continuous learning also play a significant role in helping individuals develop valuable industry skills.



📚New Knowledge and Skills Aquired

Through this project, I gained knowledge about various IT careers, professional certifications, and emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity. I also developed technical skills in HTML and CSS, webpage design, information organization, content presentation, problem-solving, and independent research. These skills have improved my understanding of both technology and web development.



🎯 Skills or Knowledge I Would Like to Develop Further

In the future, I would like to develop more advanced web development skills, including JavaScript, responsive web design, and user interface design. I am also interested in learning more about Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity because these areas are becoming increasingly important in the modern workplace.



🌱 How I Plan to Continue Learning About the Topic

I plan to continue learning by pursuing my studies on computing related courses, exploring technology-related websites, following industry news, and participating in practical projects. I am also interested in pursuing professional certifications and using online learning platforms to improve my technical and professional skills. Continuous learning will help me stay updated with new technologies and future career opportunities.



📝 Reflection on My Independent Learning Experience

This project was a valuable learning experience because it encouraged me to learn independently and solve problems on my own. Throughout the website development process, I faced several challenges, especially in webpage design and coding. However, every challenge taught me something new and improved my confidence in using HTML and CSS. Overall, this experience strengthened my technical skills, research abilities, and understanding of the importance of lifelong learning in the technology field.



Lifelong-Learning



Microsoft Learn

    What can be learned?

  • Microsoft Learn provides training in cloud computing, artificial intelligence (AI), cybersecurity, data analysis, and Microsoft technologies. It also offers learning paths for various IT careers and certifications.

  • Why is it useful?

    • The platform is free, self-paced, and provides hands-on learning resources that help learners build practical technical knowledge.

  • How can it support future learning?

    • Microsoft Learn helps learners prepare for industry-recognised certifications and develop skills needed for modern technology careers.



Coursera

  • What can be learned?

    • Coursera offers courses in Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Computer Science, Business, and many other fields from universities and industry partners.

  • Why is it useful?

    • The platform provides structured learning experiences and certificates that can help learners build professional and academic knowledge.

  • How can it support future learning?

    • Coursera allows learners to continuously develop new skills and explore career pathways through professional certificates and specialised courses.



Linkedln Learning

  • What can be learned?

    • LinkedIn Learning offers courses in technology, business, leadership, project management, communication, productivity tools, and professional development.

  • Why is it useful?

    • Courses are taught by industry experts and are designed to help learners develop workplace-relevant skills.

  • How can it support future learning?

    • It enables learners to continuously improve their technical and professional competencies while preparing for future career opportunities.



Cisco Networking Academy

  • What can be learned?

    • Cisco Networking Academy provides courses in networking, cybersecurity, data science, Python programming, Artificial Intelligence, and digital transformation.

  • Why is it useful?

    • The platform offers free learning opportunities and industry-relevant training that aligns with real-world IT careers.

  • How can it support future learning?

    • It helps learners build technical foundations and prepares them for professional certifications and technology-related careers.



freeCodeCamp

  • What can be learned?

    • freeCodeCamp teaches HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web design, databases, cybersecurity, Python, and full-stack web development.

  • Why is it useful?

    • The platform is completely free and focuses on practical, project-based learning that allows learners to apply their knowledge through real projects.

  • How can it support future learning?

    • It allows learners to continuously strengthen their programming and web development skills while building a portfolio for future career opportunities.
